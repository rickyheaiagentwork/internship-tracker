# Ricky — internship fit profile

Eternity uses this when ranking LinkedIn / careers hits.

## Hard requirements (always)

- **Summer 2027 only** — skip Fall 2026, Spring 2027, Winter, Rolling (unless the posting is explicitly Summer 2027)
- **United States only** — onsite/hybrid/remote must be US-based
- **Undergraduate (BS) only** — skip MS-only and PhD-only

## Strong yes

- AI/ML: LLMs, NLP, RL, multimodal, applied ML, ML infra / GPU
- SWE with ML flavor (platform, inference, data for ML)
- Bio-AI / computational biology / drug discovery ML (undergrad-eligible)

## Priority companies

1. NVIDIA, Google/DeepMind, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta  
2. OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere, Mistral, Hugging Face, Scale AI, W&B, Runway, Midjourney, strong YC AI startups  
3. Bio-AI: Recursion, Insitro, Schrödinger, Atomwise, Generate, Deep Genomics, Genesis, Verily, BenchSci, 10x, BioNeMo-related  

## Hard no

- Invented / unverified postings
- Fall 2026 / off-season roles
- MS-only or PhD-only roles
- Non-US locations
- Auto-submitting applications

## LinkedIn search seeds

- `internship "Summer 2027" (software OR "machine learning") "United States"`
- `intern "Summer 2027" undergrad (Amazon OR Apple OR Meta OR Google OR Microsoft OR NVIDIA)`
- `internship "Summer 2027" undergraduate (biotech OR "computational biology") "United States"`

Verify season = Summer 2027, location = US, degree = undergraduate before adding to `openings.json`.
